Talkoda logo — reference attribution

Talkoda logo uses the upright Solar music-note-2-bold icon by 480 Design (CC BY 4.0), https://github.com/480-Design/Solar-Icon-Set ; the note is recolored and scaled without rotation. Rounded brackets are redrawn from the original talkoda-icon.svg reference, based on Google Material Symbols code-rounded (Apache-2.0), https://github.com/google/material-design-icons . Color, spacing and vertical motion are adapted for Talkoda. Attribution and licenses: https://talkoda.com/brand/NOTICE.txt .
